About

From how technology works to why a business needs it.

I started in Computer Science, building robots, IoT systems and machine-learning models. That gave me a working understanding of how technology behaves in the real world.

Consulting for enterprise and government organizations in Saudi Arabia taught me the harder part. Understanding the technology is only half the equation. The real work is understanding why a business needs it, what its people are trying to achieve, and whether the proposed solution actually addresses the problem.

Today I lead Customer Success and Support at an AI-powered SaaS company, where I help organizations adopt technology, improve customer experience and strengthen operations. I'm pursuing an MBA to sharpen the business side of that bridge.
